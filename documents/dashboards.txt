PocketOps dashboards help teams watch key metrics without writing
custom queries.

A dashboard is a collection of panels. Each panel can show a line chart,
a single-number stat tile, or a small data table.

Workspace-wide dashboard filters apply to every panel on that dashboard
so viewers can narrow by team, region, or time range in one place.

Dashboards can be shared as read-only links to guest viewers. Changing
layout or adding panels requires the analyst role or higher.

PocketOps does not charge per dashboard. Usage billing is based on the
number of actively monitored hosts in the workspace.
