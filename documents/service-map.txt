Service Map is a PocketOps feature that draws a live graph of how
services connect to each other inside a workspace.

Team members open Service Map from the PocketOps web console under
Monitor → Service Map.

Before anyone can use it, a workspace admin must enable the Topology
add-on in Settings → Features.

Service Map supports filters for service name, environment
(development, staging, production), connection protocol, and health
status.

The graph refreshes automatically every 60 seconds. On the free plan,
snapshot history is kept for seven days.
